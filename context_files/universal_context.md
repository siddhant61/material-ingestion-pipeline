# Universal Context: Educational Content Pipeline

## Overall Vision

This pipeline is designed for educational content creation, following a structured workflow through multiple stages:

1. **Material Ingestion & Knowledge Graph Creation** (Current Focus)
2. **Vision & Mood-Board Creation**
3. **Summarization & Scene Outline**
4. **Storyboard Generation**
5. **Keyframe Creation**
6. **Final Assembly & Review**

## Key Design Principles

- **AI-Driven Workflow**: Use AI agents to automate and enhance content processing
- **Modular Architecture**: Each component has a specific responsibility
- **Extensible Framework**: Design for easy addition of new capabilities
- **Educational Focus**: Optimize for educational content structure and requirements

## Overall Architecture

- **Agent-Based Ecosystem**: Semi-autonomous agents working in coordination
- **Supervisor/Coordinator Pattern**: Central coordination with distributed execution
- **Knowledge Graph Foundation**: Structured representation of educational concepts
- **RAG-Based Enhancement**: Retrieval augmented generation for contextual awareness
