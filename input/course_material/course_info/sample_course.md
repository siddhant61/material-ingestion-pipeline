# Introduction to Quantum Computing

## Course Overview
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the fundamental concepts of quantum computing, quantum mechanics, and quantum algorithms. Students will learn about qubits, quantum gates, and how to implement basic quantum algorithms.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the basics of quantum mechanics as applied to computing
- Learn about quantum bits (qubits) and their properties
- Explore quantum gates and quantum circuits
- Implement simple quantum algorithms
- Understand quantum entanglement and superposition

## Learning Outcomes
After completing this course, students will be able to:
- Explain the differences between classical and quantum computing
- Design and analyze simple quantum circuits
- Implement basic quantum algorithms such as Deutsch-Jozsa and Grover's algorithm
- Understand the principles of quantum entanglement and superposition

## Subject Domain
Computer Science - Quantum Computing

## Sub-domains
- Quantum Mechanics
- Quantum Algorithms
- Quantum Information Theory
- Quantum Cryptography

## Level
Undergraduate / Graduate

## Prerequisites
- Linear Algebra
- Basic Probability Theory
- Programming experience (Python recommended)

## Duration
8 weeks, 3 hours per week

## Course Structure

### Module 1: Introduction to Quantum Computing
Introduction to the field and basic concepts
- History of quantum computing
- Quantum vs. classical computing
- Overview of quantum computing applications

### Module 2: Quantum Mechanics Basics
Fundamental quantum mechanics concepts
- Wave-particle duality
- Superposition principle
- Measurement and collapse

### Module 3: Qubits and Quantum States
Understanding quantum bits
- Single qubit systems
- Bloch sphere representation
- Multiple qubit systems

### Module 4: Quantum Gates and Circuits
Building blocks of quantum algorithms
- Single-qubit gates (Hadamard, Pauli gates)
- Two-qubit gates (CNOT, SWAP)
- Quantum circuit notation

### Module 5: Quantum Algorithms
Introduction to quantum algorithms
- Deutsch-Jozsa algorithm
- Grover's search algorithm
- Simon's algorithm
